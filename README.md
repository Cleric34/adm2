# VYUHA: EVOLUTION OF BATTLE (KURUKSHETRA)

> "Two Armies • Two Commanders • Two Vyuhas • One Battle"

A real-time 3D browser strategy war simulation game set upon the sacred plains of Kurukshetra.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

The game server is running locally on port **8080**:
👉 Open your browser to: **[http://localhost:8080/](http://localhost:8080/)**

### 1. Opening & Commander Setup
- Click **START BATTLE**
- Enter your **Commander Name**
- Select your **Army Size** (50 to 5,000 soldiers) using the slider or quick regiment presets
- Choose from **8 Authentic Ancient Indian Vyuhas**:
  1. **Padma Vyuha** (Divine Lotus — Concentric layered citadel)
  2. **Garuda Vyuha** (Celestial Eagle — Swept wings and vanguard beak)
  3. **Krauncha Vyuha** (Heron Piercer — Lethal armor-piercing wedge)
  4. **Makara Vyuha** (Armored Crocodile — Armored beast trampling jaws)
  5. **Shakata Vyuha** (Cart Bastion — Fortified rectangular mobile fortress)
  6. **Kurma Vyuha** (Tortoise Shell — Indestructible interlocking shield dome)
  7. **Ardhachandra Vyuha** (Crescent Moon — Flanking containment trap)
  8. **Chakravyuha** (Spinning Wheel of Death — 7 concentric rotating spiral rings)
- View the **interactive 2D formation visualizer** displaying actual unit coordinates in real time.
- Fine-tune your **Army Composition** across 8 unit classes:
  - Infantry, Archers, Swordsmen, Spearmen, Cavalry, Elephants, Chariots, Commanders
- Choose your **Battle Doctrine**: Aggressive, Balanced, Defensive, or Flanking.

### 2. Bot Commander Counter-Analysis & Briefing
- The AI Commander independently selects their personality (e.g. *Bhishma the Grandmaster*, *Guru Drona*, *Karna the Radiant*, *Ashwatthama the Wrathful*).
- The Bot analyzes your chosen Vyuha and selects a tactical counter formation with tailored unit composition.
- Review the **Strategic Matchup Briefing** before sounding the war horns!

### 3. Real 3D Battle Simulation
- **Battle Phases**: Continuously calculates 9 tactical battle phases from *Phase 1: Armies Advance* to *Phase 9: Battle Concluded*.
- **Realistic Combat Mechanics**:
  - Parabolic ballistic arrows flying in 3D through the air.
  - Directional flanking damage (rear & flank multipliers).
  - Spearmen anti-cavalry/beast defense bonus (+50%).
  - War Elephant trample AoE and infantry fear aura.
  - Morale routs & commander inspiration auras.
- **Commander Tactical Interventions** (Cooldown abilities):
  - *Order Cavalry Charge*
  - *Reinforce Left Flank*
  - *Reinforce Centre*
  - *Focus Archer Volley*
  - *Reform Vyuha Ranks*
  - *Sacred War Cry*
- **Camera Views**:
  - `OVERVIEW`, `ALLIED LINES`, `ENEMY LINES`, `LEFT FLANK`, `RIGHT FLANK`, `CENTRE`, `CAVALRY`, `ELEPHANTS`, `CHARIOTS`, `CINEMATIC DUEL`.
  - Free OrbitControls: Click & drag to rotate, right-click to pan, scroll wheel to zoom.
  - Click any unit on the field to open the **Unit Inspector**.

### 4. Cinematic Champion Duel (1-on-1 Clash)
- Triggers when commanders meet or via the **⚡ CINEMATIC DUEL** button.
- Transitions into a dramatic anime Kshatriya duel with curved swords, heavy spears, shields, sparks, dynamic parries, divine astral strikes, screen shake, and slow-motion decisive impact.
- The outcome delivers an immediate battlefield-wide morale surge to the victor's forces!

### 5. Final Result & Strategic Evaluation
- Real-time survivor count determines the victor.
- Detailed Strategic Scorecard evaluated across:
  - Formation Efficiency
  - Army Survival Rate
  - Formation Stability
  - Objective / Center Control
  - Combat Efficiency
  - Commander Decision Timing
- Grand Strategist Rank Award: *Maharathi*, *Atirathi*, *Rathi*, or *Senapati*.
